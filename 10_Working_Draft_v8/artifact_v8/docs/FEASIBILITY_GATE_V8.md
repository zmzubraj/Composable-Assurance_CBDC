# Research feasibility gate - v8

**Assessment date:** 29 August 2026  
**Scope:** preparation of a journal-neutral author draft and reproducibility package for a bounded methods/prototype article.  
**Excluded scope:** production certification, national deployment, regulatory effectiveness, and universal policy calibration.

## Decision

**GO** for the bounded author-draft and journal-submission preparation path.

This decision authorizes manuscript, artifact, citation, reproducibility, and venue-adaptation work using the existing formal, synthetic, laboratory-prototype, official-list-snapshot, and queueing-simulation evidence. It does not authorize stronger production, field-effectiveness, or national-scale claims. Those claims remain outside the article's evidence ceiling until their own physical, governed-data, legal, and field gates pass.

## Question and contribution boundary

The feasible paper question is whether a composable claim-to-evidence qualification method can connect reserve-settled cross-border conversion, privacy leakage and differential privacy, AML/sanctions evaluation, adaptive holding limits, and scale qualification while keeping every claim within an explicit maturity boundary.

The feasible contribution is a reproducible architecture, prototype, bounded formal analysis, synthetic and official-list benchmarks, queueing digital twin, claim-evidence matrix, and staged qualification method. It is not a claim that any isolated CBDC component is first, that the system is production certified, or that operational AML effectiveness and national capacity have been demonstrated.

## Critical path

1. Confirm the six-field research intake and freeze the canonical contribution boundary.
2. Select the primary target journal and article type; preserve a dated copy of official requirements.
3. Freeze the reproducible novelty-search package and reconcile the strongest predecessor matrix.
4. Apply venue structure, length, reference, anonymity, title-page, statement, and supplement rules.
5. Add accountable author metadata and declarations; archive the code/data package and DOI if approved.
6. Re-run tests, manuscript verifier, citation audit, visual PDF inspection, checksum verification, and target-venue checklist.
7. Obtain accountable human rendered-PDF and submission-portal approval.
