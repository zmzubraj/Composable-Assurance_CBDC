## Fit judgment

**Disposition: STRONG TOPICAL FIT, MATERIAL EDITORIAL RISK**

JFMI explicitly considers technical and policy-oriented work on payment systems, settlement systems and settlement risk, FMI interdependencies, infrastructure-related systemic risk, critical/non-bank payment service providers, retail payment infrastructures, oversight, supervision, standardization, and legislation. The manuscript directly studies reserve-settled CBDC payment infrastructure, cross-border finality, payment-service-provider boundaries, privacy/AML controls, sanctions operations, liquidity-sensitive holding limits, operational resilience, and falsifiable scale qualification.

The fit is therefore substantive, not keyword-only. The paper also matches the journal's stated academic/practitioner bridge by combining formal reasoning and computational evidence with operational qualification gates and explicit non-deployment boundaries.

## Editor-facing strengths

- The author-confirmed title uses `sovereign digital currency (CBDC)` and keeps the standard CBDC acronym explicitly discoverable.
- The contribution is framed as an assurance and qualification method for payment infrastructure, not a claim to have invented every underlying CBDC component.
- Cross-border settlement objects, quorum evidence, monetary invariants, operational failure scenarios, and qualification gates are directly relevant to payment and settlement infrastructure.
- Negative evidence is retained: the overload control fails its domestic latency target and national-scale deployment is not claimed.
- The manuscript is blinded, below the 10,000-word guidance, and provides separate editable vector figures.

## Material editorial risks

| Risk | Current evidence | Smallest adequate response |
|---|---|---|
| Breadth may look like several papers combined | Privacy, AML, sanctions, economic limits, cross-border finality and operations are all evaluated | Keep the claim-to-evidence qualification method as the single through-line; avoid adding unrelated modules. |
| Evidence maturity is internal/synthetic | No certified HSM, physical multi-region, governed institutional data or field deployment | Preserve the current evidence ceiling in title, abstract, results and conclusion; do not imply production validation. |
| High visual density | 16 figures and 22 numbered tables in 17 pages | Before final venue commitment, ask the handling editor or inspect recent JFMI articles; if necessary, move secondary sensitivity/workload material to a supplement without removing central evidence. |
| Practitioner consequence must remain obvious | Methods are technically detailed | Retain explicit operational interpretations, failure thresholds and qualification decisions adjacent to results. |
| Venue remains provisional | No final author decision | Preserve the journal-neutral master and rebuild from it if another target is selected. |

## Scope-safe contribution sentence

> This paper contributes an evidence-gated qualification framework for CBDC payment infrastructure that binds monetary invariants, cross-border settlement evidence, privacy and financial-integrity evaluation, policy limits, and operational scale claims to explicit maturity ceilings.

This sentence is a positioning statement, not a novelty verdict. Universal novelty remains unproven until a reproducible strongest-prior-art search and independent challenge are completed or the claim is narrowed accordingly.
