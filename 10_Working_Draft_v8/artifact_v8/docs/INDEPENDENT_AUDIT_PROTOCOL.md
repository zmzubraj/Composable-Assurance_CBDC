# Independent audit protocol

An independent review should cover: (1) protocol model and quorum assumptions; (2) cryptographic object formats and key lifecycle; (3) monetary-state transitions and reconciliation; (4) privacy leakage and DP implementation; (5) AML split integrity, uncertainty and adversarial testing; (6) sanctions retrieval, multilingual adjudication and ownership logic; (7) distributed performance, HSM and region-failure tests; and (8) legal-finality and data-sharing rulebooks.

The review team should be organizationally independent from the implementation team, publish a scope and version/commit, reproduce all registered claims, disclose counterexamples and severity, and verify closure of material findings before any production claim.
