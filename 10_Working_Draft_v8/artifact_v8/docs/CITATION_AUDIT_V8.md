## Residual citation limits

- The audit confirms source identity and metadata, not universal novelty or empirical truth.
- Reference 4 materially strengthens the strongest-prior-art boundary because Project Agorá now includes controlled real-value testing. The manuscript therefore preserves a narrower contribution claim: a reproducible composition-and-qualification method, not first-ever real-value CBDC settlement.
- References 40, 42 and 44 materially narrow broad “composable,” architecture-agnostic interoperability, and CBDC evaluation/verification novelty. The manuscript therefore claims a narrower cross-surface, non-compensating claim-to-evidence qualification method, not the first composable CBDC implementation, checkpoint protocol, cross-system finalization architecture, or CBDC evaluation framework.
- Reference 28 is a BIS-hosted task-force report whose own disclaimer says that its views do not necessarily reflect the BIS, CPMI, member central banks, or the full task force.
- Reference 29 is a living catalogue. A production implementation would need exact ISO 20022 message-set and schema versions rather than the generic current catalogue.
- Legal and standards references require a submission-time refresh and jurisdiction-specific interpretation before deployment claims.
