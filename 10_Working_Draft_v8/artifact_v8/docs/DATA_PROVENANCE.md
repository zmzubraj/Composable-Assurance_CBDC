# Data provenance

- `ofac_sdn.csv`, `ofac_alt.csv`, and `ofac_add.csv`: official U.S. Treasury/OFAC list exports used as a list-record sample. The benchmark creates its own held-out query population and does not contain real customer records.
- Privacy and AML transaction data are generated by the included scripts. They are synthetic and must not be represented as production or investigator evidence.
- Economic inputs are official-data anchors and published scenario envelopes identified in the manuscript. Calculations are exposure arithmetic, not estimates of causal effects.
