# Governed real-data validation protocol

## Privacy
At least three regulated PIPs; temporal holdouts; unseen customers; 10,000-candidate ranking; ledger, network, merchant, PIP and auxiliary-data attackers; cohort disparity; independent red team; secure analysis environment; no raw-data export.

## AML/CFT
Prospective or time-separated cases; PIP-local comparator; investigator-randomized or stepped-wedge workflow where lawful; alert precision, time to disposition, SAR quality, FIU feedback, disruption and false-harm review; preregistered thresholds and model changes.

## Sanctions
Institution-specific legitimate-name distribution; licensed multilingual transliteration; full secondary identifiers; direct and indirect ownership; independent analyst adjudication; queue and escalation metrics; list-version replay.

## Economics
Household/pilot microdata, bank balance sheets, funding and liquidity data, stress scenarios, distributional evidence and external model validation.
