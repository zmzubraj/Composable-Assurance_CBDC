# Production gates

No production-ready claim is permitted until all material gates pass: formal protocol verification; independent cryptographic/security/privacy audit; certified HSM and witnessed key ceremonies; physical multi-region deployment; sustained and burst load; Byzantine/key-compromise exercises; RTGS/general-ledger reconciliation; governed privacy/AML/sanctions studies; legal-finality opinions and corridor rulebook; jurisdiction-specific economic calibration; accessibility and consumer-protection pilot; incident and vendor-exit exercises.
