## User-asserted starting contract

These items are intake assertions (`E1 ASSERTED`), not verified novelty, feasibility, or evidence.

- Core research question: Under which explicitly testable assumptions can independent sovereign CBDC systems provide reserve-settled conversion, privacy-preserving financial integrity, evidence-bound cross-border finality, adaptive policy limits, and a falsifiable path to national-scale qualification?
- Novelty statement: The claimed contribution is not a new consensus primitive or first implementation of any isolated CBDC component; it is a joint claim-to-evidence qualification methodology that connects monetary invariants, settlement safety, privacy leakage and differential privacy, AML/sanctions evaluation, adaptive holding limits, and falsifiable scale gates under one maturity boundary.
- Target contribution: A reproducible system architecture, local research prototype, formal/synthetic/queueing evidence package, claim-evidence matrix, and publication-ready manuscript that defines how claims advance from analytical models to controlled prototype, physical qualification, and governed field evidence.
- Possible feasibility: Existing v8 prototype, formal model, synthetic experiments, OFAC snapshot, queueing digital twin, tests, locked Python environment, source figures/tables, and manuscript are available locally; physical multi-region, HSM-certified, governed institutional data, field validation, repository DOI, author metadata, target-venue confirmation, and final submission require external or accountable human action.
- External or real-world validation requirement: Required before any production, institutional, national-scale, or field-effectiveness claim; current manuscript excludes those claims
- Exploratory versus confirmatory analysis boundary: Existing analyses are controlled internal research evidence; confirmatory/external status must be identified per claim and cannot be upgraded by prose
- Feasibility-pilot status and authority: Existing local prototype and synthetic experiments support bounded manuscript preparation only; physical or governed field execution requires a new authorization gate
- Solution-viability status: ASSERTED ONLY; claim-matched evidence is required
- Acceptance-readiness status: NOT ASSESSABLE; editorial decisions remain external
