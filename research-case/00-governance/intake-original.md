Name of this system: Composable Assurance for Sovereign Digital Currency

Research topic name: Evidence-gated composable assurance for privacy, financial integrity, cross-border finality, adaptive policy limits, and operational qualification in sovereign CBDC systems

Core research question: Under which explicitly testable assumptions can independent sovereign CBDC systems provide reserve-settled conversion, privacy-preserving financial integrity, evidence-bound cross-border finality, adaptive policy limits, and a falsifiable path to national-scale qualification?

Novelty statement: The claimed contribution is not a new consensus primitive or first implementation of any isolated CBDC component; it is a joint claim-to-evidence qualification methodology that connects monetary invariants, settlement safety, privacy leakage and differential privacy, AML/sanctions evaluation, adaptive holding limits, and falsifiable scale gates under one maturity boundary.

The target contribution should be: A reproducible system architecture, local research prototype, formal/synthetic/queueing evidence package, claim-evidence matrix, and publication-ready manuscript that defines how claims advance from analytical models to controlled prototype, physical qualification, and governed field evidence.

Possible feasibility: Existing v8 prototype, formal model, synthetic experiments, OFAC snapshot, queueing digital twin, tests, locked Python environment, source figures/tables, and manuscript are available locally; physical multi-region, HSM-certified, governed institutional data, field validation, repository DOI, author metadata, target-venue confirmation, and final submission require external or accountable human action.
