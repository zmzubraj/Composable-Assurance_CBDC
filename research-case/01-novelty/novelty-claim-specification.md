# Novelty claim specification

## Claim ID C001

**Permitted hypothesis:** The work may be materially differentiating as a reproducible, non-compensating claim-to-evidence qualification method that connects six CBDC assurance surfaces under one explicit maturity boundary.

**Explicitly excluded priority claims:**

- first composable or standards-based CBDC implementation;
- first cross-border, atomic, multi-ledger or architecture-agnostic CBDC protocol;
- first checkpoint, reserve/lock or terminal-certificate mechanism;
- first privacy/AML compliance-by-design framework;
- first formal-method or operational-resilience treatment of CBDC;
- first CBDC prototype, real-value pilot, performance benchmark or holding-limit analysis.

## Materiality threshold

The hypothesis survives only if the contribution changes how system claims are accepted, rejected or advanced: each central claim must be linked to an explicit evidence origin and maturity ceiling, negative/limiting evidence must remain decision-bearing, and missing evidence in one assurance surface may not be offset by success in another.

## Defeating evidence

A predecessor defeats the central novelty hypothesis if, before the manuscript cutoff, it provides substantially the same cross-domain, claim-matched qualification method with explicit evidence ceilings, non-compensating gates, reproducible artifacts and comparable or stronger validation across the central assurance surfaces.
